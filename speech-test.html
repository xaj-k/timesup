Speech check

Can this device hear your child read?

Your child reads 20 words out loud, one at a time: sight words, and phonics words with the sound to practice highlighted. Each word appears once the microphone is on. The page shows what it heard, and if it didn't catch the word it listens again by itself. At the end you get the results to send back.

If your child reads a word right and the page doesn't get it, tap Said it right.

Chrome sends the audio to Google to recognize it, unless you choose to recognize on this device below. Nothing is saved after you close the page.
